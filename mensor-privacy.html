Privacy Policy for Mensor

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Mensor is a land measurement app for iPhone and iPad provided by Roviora Labs. Contact: rovioralabs@gmail.com.

Our approach

Mensor has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or third-party SDKs. We do not receive your measurements, photos, notes, or location, and we do not sell your personal information. Apple's map services, the iOS share sheet, and support email involve the separate data flows described below.

Measurements, notes, and photos

Everything you create — measurements, vertex coordinates, names, notes, colour tags, folders, and attached photos — is stored only in the app's storage on your device. The system photo picker gives the app access to only the photos you choose, not your whole library, and the app keeps its own copies inside its storage. None of this is uploaded to a developer server.

Location

Mensor uses your location to centre the map on you and to record a boundary as you walk it. By default it asks for access only while you are using the app. If you start a GPS walk and allow "Always" access, it can keep recording while your screen is locked or another app is open; recording stops when you finish or stop the walk. Location is processed on your device to calculate points, distances, and areas, and is saved only as part of measurements you save and any in-progress draft kept so you can resume. We never transmit your location to a developer server. You can change or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings; without it you can still measure by tapping points on the map.

Maps and place search

Map imagery and place search are provided by Apple Maps. When you pan, zoom, or search, your device requests map data and search results from Apple, which may include the area you are viewing, the text you type, and your IP address. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app does not attach an account or advertising identifier to these requests and does not send your saved measurements to Apple.

Exporting, importing, and sharing

Exports (KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, GPX, CSV, PDF, and share images) are created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet; they go only where you choose to send them, and that destination's own policy then applies. Files you open in the app to import are read on your device. Exported files and PDFs can contain coordinates, names, notes, and photos, so review them before sharing.

Diagnostics and support

The app does not collect crash reports, analytics, or usage statistics. It writes limited entries to Apple's on-device system log for troubleshooting; the app does not upload them. If you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us anonymised crash and usage reports. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you send, and we use it only to respond and resolve your request. You may ask us to delete that correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements.

Storage, retention, and your choices

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Settings › Data › Delete all data removes every measurement, folder, photo, and cache the app holds; deleting the app also removes its data. Device backups may include eligible app data according to your Apple settings (for example iCloud Backup); the app does not run its own cloud sync. You can manage Location and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission turns off only the feature that needs it.

Third-party services and this website

Apple, the share destinations you choose, and your device's operating system operate under their own policies. This legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to GitHub under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. We add no analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking scripts to these pages.

Children and privacy requests

Mensor is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information we hold. We hold none beyond support correspondence; we cannot access or remotely delete information stored only on your device. Contact rovioralabs@gmail.com to make a request.

Changes and contact

We will publish changes here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, email rovioralabs@gmail.com.
