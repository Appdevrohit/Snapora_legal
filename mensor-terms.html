Using the app

Mensor helps you measure land area, perimeter, distance, and radius on a map, by tapping points or walking a boundary with GPS, and to save, annotate, and export those measurements. Use it lawfully. Get permission before entering or measuring land you do not own or have a right to access. Measurements are estimates, not surveys

Results depend on GPS signal quality, map imagery, how precisely you place points, and the area calculation, which models the Earth as a sphere. They can differ from official records and from a professional survey. Do not rely on the app to establish legal boundaries, support property sales or disputes, set out construction, calculate tax or subsidy, or settle any matter where an error could cause loss or harm. Use a licensed surveyor for those. Regional units such as the bigha vary by state and country; check the value the app displays for the unit you choose. Map imagery is provided by Apple and may be out of date or offset from the real ground.
