Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

In short: your photos and videos stay on your device. Snapora has no accounts and never uploads your captures. We use your location to stamp your photos, and a few trusted services to show ads, process purchases and understand how the app is used.

1. Who we are

Snapora — GPS Camera (“Snapora”, “we”, “us”) is a camera app that stamps photos and videos with location, date and other details. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at rivoralabs@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Photos and videos. Captures are saved to your device’s photo library. We do not upload, store or view them.
  • Capture details. For each capture Snapora keeps a local record — coordinates, altitude, accuracy, address and time — so it can show your captures on the map and in History. This record never leaves your device.
  • Settings. Your preferences (template, quality, units, timestamp format) are stored on your device only.

3. Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — to take photos and record videos.
  • Microphone — to record sound with videos.
  • Photo library — to save your captures and show them in the app.
  • Location (while using the app) — to stamp your captures with where they were taken.

You can change any of these at any time in your device’s Settings. The app keeps working with any combination; features that need a missing permission simply won’t be available.

4. Information shared with service providers

  • Location for addresses. To turn your coordinates into a readable address, Snapora sends them to Google’s Geocoding service, or to Apple’s geocoder as a fallback. Only the coordinates are sent, not your photos, and we do not keep them.
  • Analytics — Google Firebase Analytics. We record how the app is used, such as which screens are opened, how long they are viewed, whether captures succeed, which templates are chosen and which permissions are granted. This is linked to a random app identifier, not to your name or contact details. It helps us fix problems and improve the app.
  • Advertising — Google AdMob. The free version shows ads. AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier to show relevant ads, but only if you allow tracking when asked. You can refuse, or change this later in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; you will then see non-personalised ads.
  • Purchases — RevenueCat and Apple. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a random app identifier so we can unlock Snapora Pro on your device.

Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

Data kept on your device is removed when you delete the app; captures already saved to your photo library remain there until you delete them. Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase for a limited period and then deleted automatically. To ask us to delete any analytics data associated with your device, email us.

6. Children

Snapora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, or to object to how it is used. Contact us at rivoralabs@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above will always show when it was last revised, and significant changes will be highlighted in the app.
