Vaultora Support

Help for Vaultora, the encrypted file vault by Roviora Labs. Email rovioralabs@gmail.com and we will reply.

Contact

Email rovioralabs@gmail.com. In the app, Settings > About > Contact Support opens a message with your app and iOS versions filled in. It contains nothing from your vault, and it is sent only if you press Send. Please do not email us your passcode or your files; we will never ask for them.

I forgot my passcode

We cannot recover it, and neither can anyone else. Your files are encrypted with keys that only your passcode unlocks, and nothing is stored anywhere else. Reset Vault in Settings erases the vault, but it needs the passcode too.

I got a new iPhone

Vaultora keeps its data out of backups and its keys on the device, so a vault cannot be moved to a new iPhone. Before you change devices, export what you want to keep (Settings > Export Vault), and import it into the new vault.

Can I see my files in Photos or Files?

No. Items you add are encrypted inside the app. Export is the way to get a normal copy out.

Does the app connect to the internet?

No. Vaultora makes no network connections. See the Privacy Policy for the details and for what is and is not encrypted.

Report a problem

Email rovioralabs@gmail.com with what you did, what you expected, what happened, and your iPhone model and iOS version.
