Privacy Policy for Glide Remote

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Glide Remote (also called Glide) is a universal TV remote app for iPhone provided by Roviora Labs. Contact: rivoralabs@gmail.com.

Our approach

The app has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, or third-party SDKs. We do not collect your remote-control activity on our servers or sell your personal information. Optional Apple services, TVs, support email, and this policy website have the separate data flows described below.

TV discovery, pairing, and control

With Local Network permission, the app discovers supported TVs using Bonjour/mDNS and SSDP and communicates with the TV you select over your network. It processes device names, network addresses, platform information, service advertisements, pairing responses, capabilities, and TV-provided app/input lists. Saved TV profiles, room names, favorites, routines, and preferences stay in app storage on your device. Pairing tokens, keys, and credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain with device-scoped protection. Commands and text you explicitly send are transmitted to the selected TV. That TV and its installed services may process them under their own policies.

Keyboard, microphone, and speech recognition

Typed or dictated text is held temporarily for composition and sent to the selected TV only when you choose Send. The app does not include that text in its diagnostics or save it as a keyboard history. Dictation requires microphone and speech-recognition permission. The app requests on-device recognition when supported. If unavailable, it asks for your consent before using Apple's network speech recognition, which can send audio and related recognition data to Apple. You can decline and keep using typed text. The app does not save voice recordings. Apple's processing is governed by its privacy disclosures: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/ask-siri-dictation/.

Photos, videos, and AirPlay

The system photo picker gives the app access to only the photos or videos you select, rather than your entire library. Selected videos may be copied into temporary app storage for playback. Selected media can be displayed using Apple's AirPlay, external-display, or system Screen Mirroring features when you choose a receiver. Media is sent to that receiver; we do not upload it to a developer server. Screen Mirroring may show other visible screen content, so choose what you display carefully. Receiver compatibility and Apple system behavior vary.

Widgets, Live Activities, and Control Center

The app and its widget extension share limited saved TV information and connection status through an on-device App Group. These surfaces may show your selected TV name or status on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can remove widgets and controls or stop Live Activities using iOS.

Diagnostics and support

The app keeps a bounded in-memory diagnostic log for troubleshooting, which resets when the app relaunches. It does not automatically upload logs. If you choose Share log, the receiving app or person gets the exported information. Review it before sharing. The app also uses Apple's system logging facilities for operational diagnostics. If you email support, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you send, and use them to respond and resolve your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements.

Storage, retention, and your choices

Saved profiles and preferences remain until you remove or reset them. Forgetting a TV removes its saved profile and requests deletion of its pairing credentials; you can also revoke the app's pairing from the TV's own settings where supported. Deleting the app removes its app container, but iOS Keychain items can survive uninstall, so forget paired TVs before uninstalling to remove those credentials. Temporary media and system-managed backups follow iOS storage and backup behavior. Device backups may include eligible app data according to your Apple settings; the app does not operate its own cloud sync. You can manage Local Network, Microphone, and Speech Recognition permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission disables the feature that needs it.

Third-party services and this website

Apple, your TV manufacturer, receiver, and any sharing destination operate under their own policies. This legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it sends ordinary web request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to GitHub under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. We add no analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking scripts to these pages. Apple's privacy policy is available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Children and privacy requests

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information we hold. Contact rivoralabs@gmail.com to make a request. We cannot access or remotely delete information held only on your iPhone or TV.

Changes and contact

We will publish changes here with an updated date. For privacy questions or requests, email rivoralabs@gmail.com.
