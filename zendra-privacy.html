Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Zendra moves files between devices you choose. We do not operate a cloud file service, require an account, or collect your files for ourselves. The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

1. Who we are

Zendra is a nearby file-sharing app provided by Roviora Labs. For privacy questions, contact rivoralabs@gmail.com.

2. Information kept on your device

Zendra stores received files, transfer history and preferences locally. Local records can include file names, file sizes, transfer status, timestamps, device names, paired-device information and settings such as appearance and accent color. These records support the features you use; they are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

3. Information shared during a transfer

When you choose to send files, Zendra shares those files directly with the receiving device or connected computer. Files may include embedded metadata, such as the location or date stored in a photo. Zendra does not automatically remove that metadata.

Nearby discovery and connection setup exchange information needed to recognize and reach devices, such as a device identifier, display name, model, network address and connection capabilities. Transfer setup exchanges file names, types, sizes and verification information. Nearby devices may see the display name you choose while the app is discoverable.

Recipients control the copies they receive. Only share files with people and devices you trust.

4. Computer sharing and network security

Computer sharing opens a temporary local web server so a browser on the same network can download files you add or upload files to your device. This feature uses plain HTTP, not HTTPS. Someone able to observe traffic on that network could see the sharing link and transferred files. Use a trusted network, keep the link private and tap Stop Sharing when you are finished.

File integrity checks help detect transfer errors; they are not a promise of end-to-end encryption. Direct Wi-Fi features depend on compatible hardware and pairing.

5. Permissions and your choices

  • Local network: used to discover nearby devices and transfer files.
  • Camera: used when you choose to scan a pairing QR code.
  • Photos and files: the system pickers let you choose items to share. Saving received photos or videos to your photo library requires the relevant permission.
  • Media library: used if you choose to share eligible, unprotected music from your library. Protected Apple Music tracks cannot be shared.

You can manage system permissions in device Settings. You choose which files to send and can decline incoming requests. If you enable automatic acceptance for previously accepted devices, eligible transfers from those devices can be accepted without another prompt. You can change this preference in Zendra Settings.

6. No advertising, tracking or developer analytics

Zendra does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your files or personal data, and we do not receive copies of your transfers. Apple may process App Store, device or diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Files and local records remain on your device until removed using the app's available management controls or by deleting the app. Copies exported to Photos, Files, another app or another device must be deleted separately. Deleting Zendra does not delete copies held by recipients.

8. Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive the address and information you include and use them to respond to your request. Do not include private files or sensitive information unless necessary. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing rivoralabs@gmail.com, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

9. Children

Zendra is a general-purpose utility. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app. A parent or guardian who has a concern about information sent to us through support can contact us at the address above.

10. This policy page

This page uses no analytics scripts, advertising or third-party fonts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, under its own privacy policy. That website hosting is separate from Zendra's local file transfers.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when Zendra's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
