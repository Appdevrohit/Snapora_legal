Privacy Policy for Vaultora

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Vaultora is an encrypted file vault for iPhone and iPad provided by Roviora Labs. Contact: rovioralabs@gmail.com.

Our approach

Vaultora makes no network connections. It has no developer-operated backend, user accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs. We do not receive your files, notes, passcode, photos or location, and we do not sell personal information. The only way anything can leave the app is by something you do: exporting or sharing a file, or sending a support email.

Your files and notes

Photos, videos, documents, audio and notes you add are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, each file under its own key, and are decrypted only in memory to show them to you. The keys exist only on your device, and your passcode is what unlocks them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What is not encrypted

File names, note titles, folder names, dates and sizes are stored as readable text in the app's database. Vaultora does not encrypt them. They are protected by your device's own data protection, which depends on your device passcode, not on your vault passcode. Anyone who can unlock your device or read its storage may be able to see this information.

The decoy vault

You can set a second passcode that opens a separate vault with its own files. It can help if someone makes you unlock the app and you give them the second passcode. It does not hide the existence of a second vault from anyone who can read your device's storage directly.

Passcode and recovery

Your passcode itself is never stored. Your vault key is kept only in wrapped form, and unwrapping it needs a key derived from your passcode. There is no account, no reset link and no copy of your keys anywhere else. If you forget your passcode, nobody, including us, can open your files. Inside the app, Reset Vault (which asks for your passcode) erases everything and starts again.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID, Vaultora asks iOS to check your face. We never see or receive biometric data; it is handled by iOS. A copy of your vault key is kept in the iOS Keychain, bound to your enrolled biometrics, and iOS invalidates it if your biometrics change.

Permissions we may ask for

Photos: you choose which photos or videos to import using the system picker, and we only read those. We can also add items to your Photos library when you export them; we never read your library for that. Camera and microphone: to capture photos and videos straight into the vault, and, if you turn on break-in alerts, to take a front-camera photo after repeated wrong passcodes. Break-in photos are kept encrypted on your device and shown only after you unlock. Location (while using the app): only if you turn it on for break-in alerts, to note roughly where the device was, rounded to about a kilometre. It stays on your device. You can change any permission in iOS Settings; denying one turns off only the feature that needs it.

Exporting and sharing

If you export, share or save a file to Photos, that copy leaves the vault: it is no longer encrypted or protected by Vaultora, and the place you send it to has its own policy. Vaultora deletes its own temporary copies when you are done.

Backups and other devices

Vaultora's data is kept out of iCloud and iTunes/Finder backups and its keys are bound to this device. There is no sync. If you lose, reset or replace your device, your vault cannot be restored on a new one. Keep your own copies of anything you cannot lose.

Diagnostics and support

Vaultora collects no crash reports, analytics or usage statistics. If you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us anonymised crash and usage reports. Contact Support opens an email for you to review; it contains the app version, your iOS version and the device model, and nothing from your vault, and it is sent only if you press Send. If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply. You can ask us to delete that correspondence.

Retention and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Reset Vault erases everything in the app; deleting the app deletes its files. iOS may keep small Keychain entries after an app is deleted; without the files they open nothing.

Children and privacy requests

Vaultora is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold. We hold none beyond support correspondence, and we cannot access or remotely delete anything stored only on your device. Email rovioralabs@gmail.com to make a request.
